# The ENERGY Suite
## Tool 6: The Power Plan

---

## Overview:

The Power Plan! The place it all comes together! If you are trying to start here: Stop. Go back. Work through tools 1-5 first. Using information derived from My Energy, the Regulator, the worksheets, and the PIC Tool, autistic or neurodivergent people and/or their teams can create a comprehensive plan for Powering UP! and Powering Down energy levels when there is a mismatch identified on the Energy Meter, or when there will be known mismatches between the person's profile and the environment or activity based on your work with the PIC. The Power Plan has three pages. The first page of the Power Plan is designed to be used as a quick visual reference for authentic and useful activity and environmental accommodations that can support a person's energy regulation, as well as provide a reminder of factors that can trigger dysregulation that should be avoided if possible. Pages two and three detail proactive regulation strategies and reactive regulation strategies. Strategies are organized by type of input (i.e., Sensory/Motor, Information/Routine, and Reflective/Forward Thinking) and their perceived effectiveness in the face of varying degrees of dysregulation (i.e., mild, moderate, extreme).

Partners seek to understand the perspective and experience of the learner they are supporting. Partners join in the learning process.
