# Sensory Affordances

## Summary

Sensory affordances are design elements and environmental features that provide opportunities for specific sensory experiences and engagement. In the context of [[neuro-inclusive-design|neuro-inclusive design]], sensory affordances are intentionally designed to support [[hypersensitivity|hypersensitive]], [[hyposensitivity|hyposensitive]], and neurotypical users through multiple sensory modalities.

## Types of Sensory Affordances

In outdoor environments, sensory affordances include:

- **Water features** — Sound masking (acoustic affordance), visual engagement, tactile opportunities, evaporative cooling
- **Vegetation and greenery** — Visual biophilic elements, olfactory variety, textural diversity, shade provision
- **Natural materials** — Tactile variety (wood, stone, bark), temperature variation, weathering and patina
- **Playground equipment** — Vestibular and proprioceptive engagement ([[sensorimotor-movement]])
- **Varied terrain** — Balance challenges, movement opportunities, visual complexity
- **Accessible seating** — Resting opportunities, social viewing, refuge positioning
- **Natural lighting** — Visual engagement without artificial flicker or glare
- **Wayfinding elements** — Navigation support reducing cognitive load and anxiety

## Critical Role in Neurodivergent Self-Regulation

From [[kelly-et-al-2025-inclusive-playgrounds|Kelly et al. (2025)]], sensory affordances are essential for neurodivergent children's self-regulation and engagement:

- **Vestibular affordances** (swinging, spinning) support arousal modulation and sustained attention through neurotransmitter release
- **Proprioceptive affordances** (climbing, resistance) provide grounding feedback and emotional regulation
- **Individual perception varies** — Same affordance may support one child's regulation while creating aversion for another; designing for sensory margins supports full spectrum
- **Sustained engagement** — When environments provide sensory affordances matching children's regulatory needs, they stay engaged longer and experience positive social outcomes

## Design Approach

[[sensory-responsive-environments-framework|SREF]] and [[inclusive-playground-design|inclusive playground design]] emphasize creating multifaceted sensory affordances through [[sensory-zoning]] to provide:

- Choice and control for users
- Support for various sensory processing styles
- Opportunities for both stimulation and calm
- Accessibility for multiple ages and ability levels
