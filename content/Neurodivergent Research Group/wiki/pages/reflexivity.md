# Reflexivity

## Summary

A methodological practice through which scholars critically reflect on their own positionality and how it impacts participants and co-researchers, understanding of the topic, and the knowledge produced. In [[participatory-action-research|PAR]], critical reflexivity is a key counter-strategy for addressing power inequalities and risks of co-option.

## Key claims

- Reflexivity requires scholars to examine how their positionality (social position, identity, disciplinary training, institutional affiliations) shapes the research process and its outcomes [[raw/Participatory action research - Nature Reviews Methods Primers.md|Cornish et al.]]
- Critical reflexivity is one of five counter-strategies recommended for PAR challenges, alongside building PAR-friendly networks, cultivating a critical community, redistributing powers, and trusting the process [[raw/Participatory action research - Nature Reviews Methods Primers.md|Cornish et al.]]
- Reflexivity extends beyond individual self-examination to collective processes where research teams critically assess power dynamics, assumptions, and knowledge production practices [[raw/Participatory action research - Nature Reviews Methods Primers.md|Cornish et al.]]
- In CBPR, reflexivity means becoming aware of, critically examining, and owning one's privilege, power, and patterns of intentional and unintentional classism and racism; understanding intersecting positionalities in relation to community partners is essential for authentic power-sharing (Muhammad et al. 2015) [[raw/Community-based Participatory Research (CBPR)_ Towards Equitable Involvement of Community in Psychology Research.md|Collins et al.]]
- Reflexivity is listed as the first implementation step for CBPR in psychology, to be practiced prior to and throughout the entire research process [[raw/Community-based Participatory Research (CBPR)_ Towards Equitable Involvement of Community in Psychology Research.md|Collins et al.]]
- In neurodiversity research, Bernard et al. explicitly recommend that neurotypical research team members critically reflect on how their standpoints influence the research and their collaboration — framing reflexivity as an essential team practice, not merely an individual scholarly virtue [[raw/Not your "typical" research_ Inclusion ethics in neurodiversity scholarship _ Industrial and Organizational Psychology|Bernard et al. 2023]]
- In a [[chinn-balota-2024-photovoice-intellectual-disabilities|systematic review of 31 photovoice studies]] with people with intellectual disabilities, researcher reflexivity received little consideration despite being a key aspect of qualitative research; only Dorozenko et al. (2015), Krisson et al. (2021), and Povee et al. (2014) used reflexive journals to examine how ableist assumptions impacted their research processes [[raw/A systematic review of photovoice research methods with people with intellectual disabilities.md|Chinn & Balota]]
