# Post-Occupancy Evaluation

## Summary

Post-Occupancy Evaluation (POE) is a systematic assessment of a built environment after it has been occupied and in use, comparing actual performance against design intent. In autism design research, POE serves both as a diagnostic tool identifying needed interventions and as an outcome measure evaluating their efficacy after implementation.

## Use in ASPECTSS Research

[[magda-mostafa|Mostafa]] uses POE as the primary applied vehicle for the [[aspectss-design-index|ASPECTSS]] framework:

1. **Diagnostic POE** — assess an existing building against ASPECTSS criteria to identify priority design interventions
2. **Post-intervention POE** — evaluate outcomes of implemented interventions, typically using staff/occupant surveys, structured observations, and interviews

The 2018 POE at Els for Autism (Jupiter, FL) identified five intervention priorities: colour-coded navigation, acoustical treatments, transition alcoves, classroom compartmentalization, and escape/de-escalation spaces. Outcomes were assessed 18 months post-implementation via the methods described in [[mostafa-et-al-2023-aspectss-case-study]].

## Triangulation Approach

Mostafa's POE methodology for autism school design uses three instruments in combination:

- **Surveys** — staff and teacher Likert-scale and open-ended questions; provide scalable perception data
- **Structured observations** — direct behavioral data; conducted through one-way glass to minimise observer influence
- **Thematic interviews** — probing and contextualising quantitative findings; capturing emergent or unexpected uses

Triangulation serves three purposes: (1) increasing result reliability; (2) ensuring staff/faculty alignment with design intent (critical for operational fidelity); (3) identifying remaining research gaps [[mostafa-et-al-2023-aspectss-case-study]].

## Limitations in Autism Design Contexts

- Teacher/staff reports inevitably influenced by awareness of being studied
- Staff are proxies for student experience; direct autistic input absent in existing studies
- POE of a single site requires replication before generalising
- Fidelity gap: whether staff actually implement design templates affects outcomes (only 50% uptake in 2023 case study)
