# GRIPP2

## Summary

Guidance for Reporting Involvement of Patients and the Public, version 2 (GRIPP2) is an Equator Network reporting checklist developed by Staniszewska et al. (2017) providing a standardized series of concepts to consider and report when incorporating patient and public involvement (PPI) in research. It addresses methods of involvement, intensity of engagement, and impact reporting.

## Key claims

- GRIPP2 is the primary reporting checklist for patient and public involvement in research, published in *BMJ* (Staniszewska et al. 2017) [[raw/Evaluation of research co-design in health_ a systematic overview of reviews and development of a framework.md|Peters et al.]]
- The [[co-design-evaluation-framework]] complements rather than replaces GRIPP2; it can be used prospectively to plan evaluation or retrospectively to inform GRIPP2 completion [[raw/Evaluation of research co-design in health_ a systematic overview of reviews and development of a framework.md|Peters et al.]]
- End-user engagement in health research has an Equator reporting checklist, reflecting the growing institutionalization of participatory approaches [[raw/Evaluation of research co-design in health_ a systematic overview of reviews and development of a framework.md|Peters et al.]]
- GRIPP and GRIPP2 are generic guidelines not specific to co-design in [[digital-health-interventions]]; standardized digital-health-specific co-design reporting guidelines are needed, ideally co-created with patients and caregivers [[raw/An umbrella review on how digital health intervention co-design is conducted and described - npj Digital Medicine.md|Kilfoy et al.]]
- The GRIPP checklist (predecessor to GRIPP2) was cited in a rapid overview of 23 reviews as an emerging reporting standard for co-design activities, alongside calls for much better description of what co-design actually involves in practice [[raw/Research co-design in health_ a rapid overview of reviews - Health Research Policy and Systems.md|Slattery et al.]]
