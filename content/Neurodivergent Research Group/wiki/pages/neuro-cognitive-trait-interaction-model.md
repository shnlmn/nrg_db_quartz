# Neuro-Cognitive Trait Interaction Model

**Summary** — The Neuro-Cognitive Trait Interaction Model (NCTIM) is an original methodological framework for inclusive research design developed by [[jessica-dark|Jessica Dark]] (2025). It provides a structured yet flexible approach for researchers working with neurodivergent participant groups, moving beyond diagnostic labels to center how cognitive processing traits shape research participation. NCTIM follows a three-stage process: mapping study demands, identifying potential trait interactions, and embedding responsive design features.

## Key claims

- NCTIM offers a distinctive contribution to inclusive research design by explicitly recognizing how neuro-cognitive traits shape people's experiences within research environments [[raw/dark-2025-inclusion-by-design-a-neuro-cognitive-trait-interaction-approach-to-neurodivergent-research.extracted.md|Dark 2025]]
- The model is grounded in the [[neurodiversity-paradigm]], [[epistemic-justice]], [[epistemic-enablement]], and [[relational-inclusion]], creating ethical and philosophical foundations for inclusion [[raw/dark-2025-inclusion-by-design-a-neuro-cognitive-trait-interaction-approach-to-neurodivergent-research.extracted.md|Dark 2025]]
- NCTIM focuses on neuro-cognitive traits—[[communication-preferences]], [[executive-functioning]], [[monotropic-attention]], [[sensory-processing-autism]]—rather than diagnostic categories (autism, ADHD, dyslexia) to inform responsive research design [[raw/dark-2025-inclusion-by-design-a-neuro-cognitive-trait-interaction-approach-to-neurodivergent-research.extracted.md|Dark 2025]]

### Stage 1: Mapping Study Demands

- The first stage involves understanding the study's goals, methods, data collection procedures, timeline, communication formats, and fixed institutional/ethical/epistemological constraints [[raw/dark-2025-inclusion-by-design-a-neuro-cognitive-trait-interaction-approach-to-neurodivergent-research.extracted.md|Dark 2025]]
- Clarifying which aspects of the research are fixed vs. adaptable enables researchers to anticipate potential barriers and consider where adaptations are required [[raw/dark-2025-inclusion-by-design-a-neuro-cognitive-trait-interaction-approach-to-neurodivergent-research.extracted.md|Dark 2025]]

### Stage 2: Identifying Neuro-Cognitive Trait Interactions

- This stage involves identifying key neuro-cognitive traits associated with the participant demographic and reflecting on how they might shape participants' experiences of the study [[raw/dark-2025-inclusion-by-design-a-neuro-cognitive-trait-interaction-approach-to-neurodivergent-research.extracted.md|Dark 2025]]
- The analysis examines whether aspects of the research design might unintentionally create barriers; for example, time-limited interviews may challenge people who need longer to recall information or organize thoughts [[raw/dark-2025-inclusion-by-design-a-neuro-cognitive-trait-interaction-approach-to-neurodivergent-research.extracted.md|Dark 2025]]
- Neuro-cognitive traits are recognized as varied, dynamic, and context-dependent; researchers reflect on how specific traits influence participants' engagement with study activities and structures [[raw/dark-2025-inclusion-by-design-a-neuro-cognitive-trait-interaction-approach-to-neurodivergent-research.extracted.md|Dark 2025]]

### Stage 3: Embedding Responsive Features

- Building on insights from earlier stages, researchers embed responsive features that reduce barriers, enhance clarity, and support sustained engagement throughout the research process [[raw/dark-2025-inclusion-by-design-a-neuro-cognitive-trait-interaction-approach-to-neurodivergent-research.extracted.md|Dark 2025]]
- Responsive features may include multimodal materials (written + audio), clear language with visual markers, flexible response formats (verbal or text), personalized interview schedules, sensory breaks, and ongoing consent processes [[raw/dark-2025-inclusion-by-design-a-neuro-cognitive-trait-interaction-approach-to-neurodivergent-research.extracted.md|Dark 2025]]
- The [[eight-principles-of-neuro-inclusion]] (Dark, 2024) emphasizing autonomy, trust, and epistemic respect help translate inclusive intentions into tangible design features [[raw/dark-2025-inclusion-by-design-a-neuro-cognitive-trait-interaction-approach-to-neurodivergent-research.extracted.md|Dark 2025]]

## Application Example: Autistic Women in Workplace Diagnosis Disclosure

- Dark applied NCTIM to a pilot study exploring autistic women's experiences of diagnosis disclosure in the workplace, adapting research design despite disciplinary constraints (organizational psychology favors quantitative methods) [[raw/dark-2025-inclusion-by-design-a-neuro-cognitive-trait-interaction-approach-to-neurodivergent-research.extracted.md|Dark 2025]]
- Design featured: two-part pilot (written responses + online interview); personalized interview schedules using pre-interview written responses; accommodations for communication preferences, sensory needs, and memory recall; scheduled sensory breaks; ongoing consent; pre-written withdrawal statement; post-interview support signposting [[raw/dark-2025-inclusion-by-design-a-neuro-cognitive-trait-interaction-approach-to-neurodivergent-research.extracted.md|Dark 2025]]
- Participants mirrored the relational care during interviews by checking in with the researcher, reflecting an ethic of mutual understanding and neuro-affirming connection [[raw/dark-2025-inclusion-by-design-a-neuro-cognitive-trait-interaction-approach-to-neurodivergent-research.extracted.md|Dark 2025]]

## Positioning Within Inclusive Research Design Frameworks

- NCTIM complements [[universal-design-for-learning|UDL]] (flexibility in learning/engagement) by extending beyond educational contexts and adding relational, sensory, and emotional dimensions [[raw/dark-2025-inclusion-by-design-a-neuro-cognitive-trait-interaction-approach-to-neurodivergent-research.extracted.md|Dark 2025]]
- NCTIM builds on the [[social-model-of-disability]] (systemic barrier focus) with a detailed, trait-informed framework supporting researchers to respond to what neurodivergent participants experience during research [[raw/dark-2025-inclusion-by-design-a-neuro-cognitive-trait-interaction-approach-to-neurodivergent-research.extracted.md|Dark 2025]]
- NCTIM is complementary to [[participatory-research]] (co-production): NCTIM can act as a preparatory tool to adapt research settings before participatory models are implemented [[raw/dark-2025-inclusion-by-design-a-neuro-cognitive-trait-interaction-approach-to-neurodivergent-research.extracted.md|Dark 2025]]
- Unlike purely structural approaches (universal design, disability models), NCTIM uniquely centers relational responsiveness to how neurodivergent people process, interact, and communicate [[raw/dark-2025-inclusion-by-design-a-neuro-cognitive-trait-interaction-approach-to-neurodivergent-research.extracted.md|Dark 2025]]

## Limitations and Future Considerations

- Currently best suited to relational, inclusive, participatory research environments where flexibility, dialogue, and relational reciprocity can be embedded [[raw/dark-2025-inclusion-by-design-a-neuro-cognitive-trait-interaction-approach-to-neurodivergent-research.extracted.md|Dark 2025]]
- Challenges in quantitative, clinical, or institutional settings with standardized procedures and limited researcher-participant interaction; core features such as co-regulated pacing and sensory awareness may be harder to implement [[raw/dark-2025-inclusion-by-design-a-neuro-cognitive-trait-interaction-approach-to-neurodivergent-research.extracted.md|Dark 2025]]
- Risk that neuro-cognitive traits may be misconstrued as diagnostic markers or applied reductively; NCTIM is not a diagnostic tool but a relational framework promoting inclusion through thoughtful, embodied design [[raw/dark-2025-inclusion-by-design-a-neuro-cognitive-trait-interaction-approach-to-neurodivergent-research.extracted.md|Dark 2025]]
- Trait-informed principles remain relevant as guides for context-sensitive adaptation even in less flexible settings, supporting ethical reflection across diverse methodological contexts [[raw/dark-2025-inclusion-by-design-a-neuro-cognitive-trait-interaction-approach-to-neurodivergent-research.extracted.md|Dark 2025]]
