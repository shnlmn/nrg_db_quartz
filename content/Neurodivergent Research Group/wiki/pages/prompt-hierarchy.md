# Prompt Hierarchy

## Summary

The prompt hierarchy is a least-to-most prompting sequence for supporting AAC use: begin with modeling and wait time, then indirect visual cues (e.g., moving the device closer, gesturing toward it), before considering any physical prompt. Physical prompting should be minimized because it risks learned helplessness and loss of bodily autonomy; hand-under-hand (where the student remains in control of their own body) is preferred over hand-over-hand, and student consent is required before any physical assistance.

## Key claims

- The least amount of prompting should be provided, with wait time first, before further indirect visual or verbal cues. [[raw/AAC_and_Autism_Tip_Sheet_Edu.extracted.md]]
- Physical prompts should be minimized as much as possible: they can lead to learned helplessness and a lack of bodily autonomy. [[raw/AAC_and_Autism_Tip_Sheet_Edu.extracted.md]]
- Student consent must be obtained prior to any type of physical assistance. [[raw/AAC_and_Autism_Tip_Sheet_Edu.extracted.md]]
- Hand-under-hand prompting allows the student to remain in control of their own body and is preferred over hand-over-hand prompting. [[raw/AAC_and_Autism_Tip_Sheet_Edu.extracted.md]]
