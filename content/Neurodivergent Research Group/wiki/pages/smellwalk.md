# Smellwalk

## Summary

A smellwalk is a qualitative research method in which naive study participants are guided through an in-situ location to measure and describe environmental odours and olfactory experiences. It is an exploratory approach to characterising the sensory experience and olfactory qualities of places.

## Key claims

- Smellwalk and smellscape are exploratory concepts that have been used to measure and describe the sensory experience of environmental odours [[raw/Sensory characteristics of a place_ The development of the sensory walk questionnaire|Pohjanheimo et al.]]
- Smellwalks have been applied in rural planning, design of the built environment, therapeutic contexts (stress reduction, well-being), experiential tourism, and heritage/cultural value assessment [[raw/Sensory characteristics of a place_ The development of the sensory walk questionnaire|Pohjanheimo et al.]]
- Smellwalks involve naive participants in situ (in the actual location) to capture authentic experiences of environmental odours [[raw/Sensory characteristics of a place_ The development of the sensory walk questionnaire|Pohjanheimo et al.]]
- Understanding and measuring the perceptual quality of smellscapes is needed to guide the design of smell environments [[raw/Sensory characteristics of a place_ The development of the sensory walk questionnaire|Pohjanheimo et al.]]
- Smellscapes can be managed and controlled via separation, deodorization, masking, and scenting methods, but management requires smell identification and understanding of multi-sensory perception [[raw/Sensory characteristics of a place_ The development of the sensory walk questionnaire|Pohjanheimo et al.]]
