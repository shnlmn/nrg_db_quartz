# NAIT Sensory Checklist

## Summary

The NAIT Sensory Checklist is an informal assessment tool developed by NAIT (National Autism Initiative Team) to evaluate [[sensory-preferences]] in adults undergoing neurodevelopmental assessment. It provides a structured but flexible framework for self-assessment or clinician-administered inquiry across six sensory domains.

## Key claims

- Designed for completion as part of adult neurodevelopmental assessment for [[sensory-processing-autism]], [[sensory-processing-adhd]], and other neurodevelopmental conditions [[raw/NAIT-Guide-to-Assessment-of-Sensory-Preferences-in-Adults-2022.extracted.md]]
- Assesses six primary sensory domains: Movement, Touch/Temperature, Auditory, Vision, Olfactory, and Gustatory [[raw/NAIT-Guide-to-Assessment-of-Sensory-Preferences-in-Adults-2022.extracted.md]]
- Can be completed via self-assessment or as part of clinical interview [[raw/NAIT-Guide-to-Assessment-of-Sensory-Preferences-in-Adults-2022.extracted.md]]
- Flexible tool designed for use by all clinicians in multidisciplinary neurodevelopmental assessment teams [[raw/NAIT-Guide-to-Assessment-of-Sensory-Preferences-in-Adults-2022.extracted.md]]
- For each sensory domain, prompts documentation of: activities/sensations strongly liked, activities used to reduce stress/relax, activities/sensations strongly disliked, and impact on daily functioning [[raw/NAIT-Guide-to-Assessment-of-Sensory-Preferences-in-Adults-2022.extracted.md]]

## Six sensory domains

1. **Movement** — includes examples of vestibular and kinesthetic activities (cycling, running, dancing, rocking, stretching, etc.)
2. **Touch and Temperature** — includes tactile, proprioceptive, and thermal experiences (weighted items, texture, baths, massage, etc.)
3. **Auditory/Listening** — includes acoustic and musical experiences (quiet, sounds of nature, music, white noise, etc.)
4. **Vision/Looking** — includes visual and lighting preferences (images, nature, colors, lighting intensity, etc.)
5. **Olfactory/Smelling** — includes smell preferences and aversions (scents, cooking, natural smells, etc.)
6. **Gustatory/Tasting/Chewing** — includes taste and oral motor activities (textures, flavors, chewing, sucking, etc.)

## Reframing diagnostic criteria

The NAIT guide emphasizes positive language reframing of sensory diagnostic indicators:

| DSM-5/ICD-11 Language | Positive Reframing |
|---|---|
| Stereotypies/Motor movements | Self-regulation using touch or movement |
| Fidgets/Squirms | Self-regulation techniques for calmness |
| Insistence on sameness | Predictability and desirability in routines |
| Restricted/Fixed interests | Sensory preferences related to interests and motivation |
| Hypo/Hypersensitivity to sensory stimuli | Specific sensory preferences in different environments |
