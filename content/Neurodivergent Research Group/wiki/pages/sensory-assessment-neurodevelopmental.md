# Sensory Assessment in Neurodevelopmental Evaluation

## Summary

Systematic evaluation of [[sensory-preferences]] and [[sensory-processing-dysfunction]] as a core component of adult neurodevelopmental assessment for [[sensory-processing-autism]], [[sensory-processing-adhd]], and other neurodevelopmental conditions. Assessment informs diagnosis, intervention planning, and environmental accommodations.

## Key claims

- [[sensory-processing-autism]]: Sensory reactivity is one of the core diagnostic criteria for ASD in DSM-5 and ICD-11 [[raw/NAIT-Guide-to-Assessment-of-Sensory-Preferences-in-Adults-2022.extracted.md]]
- [[sensory-processing-adhd]]: Sensory differences are important diagnostic criteria for ADHD, including movement-seeking, fidgeting, and sensory environment effects on regulation, attention, and concentration [[raw/NAIT-Guide-to-Assessment-of-Sensory-Preferences-in-Adults-2022.extracted.md]]
- A mismatch between sensory preferences and physical/social environments is associated with poorer outcomes and distress [[raw/NAIT-Guide-to-Assessment-of-Sensory-Preferences-in-Adults-2022.extracted.md]]
- Assessment approaches include: direct observation, environmental assessment, and self or proxy reports [[raw/NAIT-Guide-to-Assessment-of-Sensory-Preferences-in-Adults-2022.extracted.md]]
- [[occupational-therapy-visual-impairment|Occupational therapists]] are key team members with expertise in sensory-environment-occupation relationships; however, assessment can be conducted by other team members with appropriate knowledge and skills [[raw/NAIT-Guide-to-Assessment-of-Sensory-Preferences-in-Adults-2022.extracted.md]]

## Assessment tools

### Formal Assessment Tools
- [[adult-adolescent-sensory-profile]] (AASP) — standardized quantitative tool via Pearson Clinical
- [[adult-sensory-history]] (ASH) — standardized history-taking tool via The Spiral Foundation
- [[sensory-profile-2]] (SP-2) — standardized occupational therapy assessment across the lifespan

### Informal Assessment Tools
- [[nait-sensory-checklist]] — flexible self or clinician-administered checklist across six sensory domains
- SPD (Sensory Processing Disorder) Checklist — adult questions available
- Sensory Challenge Questionnaire
- Sensory Diet Questionnaire
- Autism-Education-Trust Sensory Assessment Checklist
- Multidimensional Assessment of Interoceptive Awareness

## Rationale and benefits

Understanding [[sensory-preferences]] helps individuals:
- Anticipate situations where they may feel overwhelmed
- Develop strategies to reduce overwhelming stimuli
- Engage in activities that promote calm and regulation [[raw/NAIT-Guide-to-Assessment-of-Sensory-Preferences-in-Adults-2022.extracted.md]]

Clinical benefits:
- Reframe deficit-focused diagnostic language into strengths-based language [[raw/NAIT-Guide-to-Assessment-of-Sensory-Preferences-in-Adults-2022.extracted.md]]
- Inform environmental accommodations and adaptations
- Support enhanced daily functioning and participation at home, work, education, and community [[raw/NAIT-Guide-to-Assessment-of-Sensory-Preferences-in-Adults-2022.extracted.md]]
