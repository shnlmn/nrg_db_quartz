# Ruth Baumeister

## Summary

Ruth Baumeister is an architect and Associate Professor of Theory and History at the Aarhus School of Architecture (Denmark). She co-chaired the Design for Inclusivity science panel at the UIA World Congress of Architects 2023 in Copenhagen alongside [[magda-mostafa|Magda Mostafa]].

## Role and Affiliations

- Associate Professor of Theory and History — Aarhus School of Architecture
- Co-chair, Design for Inclusivity panel — [[uia-world-congress-2023|UIA World Congress of Architects 2023]]
