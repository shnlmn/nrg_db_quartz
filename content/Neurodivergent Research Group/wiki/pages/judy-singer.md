# Judy Singer

## Summary

Judy Singer is an Australian sociologist credited with coining the term "neurodiversity" — a concept covering the natural variation between human brains, encompassing both atypical and neurotypical minds.

## Key claims

- First proposed the concept of neurodiversity as a unifying term for the full spectrum of human brain variation [[raw/Designkriterier til arkitektur, der rummer autisme|rumsans.dk 2026]]
- Neurodiversity frames variation between brains as natural, not pathological — living with a neurodivergent brain is not inherently a disadvantage if one's social and physical environment accommodates it [[raw/Designkriterier til arkitektur, der rummer autisme|rumsans.dk 2026]]
