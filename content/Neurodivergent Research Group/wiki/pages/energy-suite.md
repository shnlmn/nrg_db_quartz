# Energy Suite

## Summary

The ENERGY Suite is a set of six sequential tools for helping autistic and neurodivergent people (and their support teams) understand and regulate their energy levels. Tools 1–5 build a personal profile of energy signals, triggers, and preferred regulation strategies; Tool 6, [[power-plan]], synthesizes that profile into an actionable plan. The suite is co-authored by Laurent & Fede (2025).

## Key claims

- The suite is explicitly sequential: users are directed to complete Tools 1–5 before attempting Tool 6, [[power-plan]] ([[raw/0825_PowerPlan_Post.extracted]]).
- Named component tools referenced by the Power Plan include [[my-energy]] (an "Energy Meter" for identifying mismatches between energy level and context), [[the-regulator]] (a strategies/"Power Packs" resource), and [[pic-tool]] (used to identify triggers, challenging contexts, and environmental accommodations) ([[raw/0825_PowerPlan_Post.extracted]]).
- Additional referenced-but-undetailed worksheets: "Developmentally Accessible Regulation Strategy Worksheets," "MR/SR Worksheet," and the "All Day Long, All Day Strong Strategy Worksheet" ([[raw/0825_PowerPlan_Post.extracted]]).
