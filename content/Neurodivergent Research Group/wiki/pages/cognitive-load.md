# Cognitive Load

## Summary

Cognitive load refers to the amount of mental effort and working memory capacity required to process and comprehend information or complete a task. In educational and research contexts, it is particularly relevant to understanding how neurodivergent individuals experience and manage information processing demands, especially in online environments.

## Key claims

- The relationship between cognitive load and neurodiversity in online education is a researchable area worthy of empirical investigation [[raw/Co-Design for Participatory Neurodiversity Research_ Collaborating With a Community Advisory Board to Design a Research Study _ Published in Journal of Participatory Resea.md|Le Cunff et al.]]
- Reducing cognitive overwhelm in participatory research materials can be achieved through accessibility strategies such as providing explanatory video walkthroughs of documents in addition to text, breaking content into digestible sections with bullet points, and allowing flexibility in document format [[raw/Co-Design for Participatory Neurodiversity Research_ Collaborating With a Community Advisory Board to Design a Research Study _ Published in Journal of Participatory Resea.md|Le Cunff et al.]]
