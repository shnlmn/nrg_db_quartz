# Alexithymia

## Summary

Alexithymia is a condition prevalent among neurodivergent individuals that makes it challenging to identify and articulate emotions through language. It has significant implications for research methodology, as traditional questions like "How are you feeling?" may not yield accurate responses from participants with alexithymia.

## Key claims

- Researchers should provide alternative mediums for emotional expression, such as art, descriptions of mental images, or descriptions of physical sensations [[raw/3 tips for designing user research for neurodivergent testers|AbilityNet]]
- Emotional expression should not be limited to a predefined set of options; participants need time to respond authentically [[raw/3 tips for designing user research for neurodivergent testers|AbilityNet]]
