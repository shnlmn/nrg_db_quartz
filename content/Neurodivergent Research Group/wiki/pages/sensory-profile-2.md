# Sensory Profile 2

## Summary

The Sensory Profile 2 (SP-2) is a standardized occupational therapy assessment for measuring [[sensory-processing-dysfunction]] across the lifespan. It is the most widely used and validated sensory processing measure in clinical and school-based OT practice, accepted by Medicaid, IDEA programs, and commercial payers. Six forms serve different age ranges from infancy through adulthood, allowing comparison of sensory patterns across developmental stages and settings (home vs. school).

## Forms and Age Ranges

| Form | Age Range | Items | Time | Rater | Primary Clinical Use |
|------|-----------|-------|------|-------|---------------------|
| **Infant Sensory Profile 2 (ISP-2)** | Birth–6 months | 25 | 5–10 min | Primary caregiver | NICU follow-up; feeding difficulties; sleep dysregulation; early developmental concerns |
| **Toddler Sensory Profile 2 (TSP-2)** | 7–35 months | 54 | 10–15 min | Primary caregiver | Early intervention eligibility; picky eating; ASD screening support; preschool readiness |
| **Child Sensory Profile 2 (CSP-2)** | 3:0–14:11 years | 86 | 15–20 min | Primary caregiver | IEP goal development; ASD/ADHD evaluation support; school accommodations; sensory diet planning |
| **Short Sensory Profile 2 (SSP-2)** | 3:0–14:11 years | 34 | 5–10 min | Primary caregiver | Screening and re-evaluation; clinical trials; population-level pediatric screening |
| **School Companion Sensory Profile 2 (SCSP-2)** | 3:0–14:11 years | 94 | 15–20 min | Classroom teacher | Cross-setting comparison (home vs. school); IDEA documentation; MSP billing support; generates school-based sensory factors |
| **Adolescent/Adult Sensory Profile (A/ASP)** | 11 years and older | 60 | 10–15 min | Self-report | Adult OT evaluations; mental health; workplace ergonomics; adolescent ASD support |

## Key Features

- **Quadrant framework:** Organizes scores into [[sensory-quadrants]], allowing practitioners to understand not just that sensory differences exist, but the specific pattern and mechanism (under-responsivity, seeking, sensitivity, avoidance).
- **Multi-setting analysis:** CSP-2 and SCSP-2 together enable comparison of home and school sensory patterns, supporting IDEA documentation and identifying environmental accommodations.
- **Billing and documentation context:** Recognized by major payers; sensory-based OT interventions are documentarily supported when SP-2 scores establish sensory processing differences.
- **Diagnostic relevance:** [[sensory-processing-autism|Evidence-based connection to ASD diagnosis]] via DSM-5 Criterion B; useful in [[sensory-processing-adhd|ADHD]] and [[sensory-processing-anxiety|anxiety]] evaluations for differential diagnosis.

## Clinical Limitations

Practitioners must communicate to families and referral sources that:
- SP-2 is a **behavioral screening and outcome measure**, not a diagnostic instrument
- Scores document sensory patterns and guide intervention planning; they do not diagnose autism, ADHD, or anxiety
- Cross-setting analysis (home vs. school) can identify environmental contributions vs. child-intrinsic factors, but clinical judgment is required for interpretation
