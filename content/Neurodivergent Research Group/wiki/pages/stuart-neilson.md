# Stuart Neilson

## Summary

Stuart Neilson produced and uploaded the walkthrough video of [[magda-mostafa|Magda Mostafa]]'s Venice Biennale 2023 installation "A Case for Sensory Decolonisation: Autistic Escape" (Palazzo Mora, European Cultural Centre). The video was published 2023-11-29 on Vimeo under a Creative Commons Attribution No Derivatives licence.
