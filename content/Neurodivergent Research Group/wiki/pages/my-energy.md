# My Energy

## Summary

My Energy is Tool 1 (or an early tool) in the [[energy-suite]], centered on an "Energy Meter" that identifies mismatches between a person's energy level and the demands of their environment or activity. It is referenced by [[power-plan]] as the source for known triggers, challenging contexts, and signals that support is needed, but its own content has not yet been ingested as a primary source.

## Key claims

- The Power Plan is triggered "when a mismatch [is] identified on the Energy Meter" — the Energy Meter is My Energy's core instrument ([[raw/0825_PowerPlan_Post.extracted]]).
- [[power-plan]] directs users to reference "My Energy/Signals of Dysregulation" when documenting signals that support is needed ([[raw/0825_PowerPlan_Post.extracted]]).
