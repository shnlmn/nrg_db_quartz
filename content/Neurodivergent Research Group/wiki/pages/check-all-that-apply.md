# Check-All-That-Apply

## Summary

Check-All-That-Apply (CATA) is a questionnaire method in which respondents select all descriptors from a provided list that apply to their experience of a product, environment, or stimulus. It is used in sensory science and consumer research to characterise multi-dimensional experiences in a structured, quantitative way.

## Key claims

- The [[sensory-walk-questionnaire]] uses a CATA question built from a multi-sensory lexicon of descriptors across sound, smell, vision, and somatosensory modalities [[raw/Sensory characteristics of a place_ The development of the sensory walk questionnaire|Pohjanheimo et al.]]
- CATA is one of the established multi-sensory evaluation practices adapted to place and environment assessment [[raw/Sensory characteristics of a place_ The development of the sensory walk questionnaire|Pohjanheimo et al.]]
- CATA questions can reduce the cognitive load of free-text generation while still capturing multi-dimensional sensory experience, making them potentially valuable for accessible research with neurodivergent participants [[raw/3 tips for designing user research for neurodivergent testers|formatting-building-experience-questions page]]
