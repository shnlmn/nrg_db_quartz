# Power Plan

## Summary

The Power Plan is Tool 6 of the [[energy-suite]], the capstone tool where information from [[my-energy]], [[the-regulator]], the regulation-strategy worksheets, and the [[pic-tool]] is synthesized into a comprehensive plan for "Powering UP!" and "Powering Down" a person's energy levels. It is used when a mismatch is identified on the Energy Meter, or when a known mismatch between a person's profile and an environment/activity is anticipated. Users are instructed to complete Tools 1–5 before starting the Power Plan. The plan spans three pages: a quick-reference summary of accommodations and dysregulation triggers, and two pages of leveled proactive/reactive regulation strategies.

## Key claims

- Page 1 is a quick visual reference listing authentic, individualized activity/environmental accommodations that support regulation, plus triggers that should be avoided where possible ([[raw/0825_PowerPlan_Post.extracted]]).
- Pages 2–3 detail proactive and reactive regulation strategies, organized along two axes: input type (Sensory/Motor, Information/Routine, Reflective/Forward Thinking) and degree of dysregulation (mild, moderate, extreme) ([[raw/0825_PowerPlan_Post.extracted]]).
- The tool's essential-knowledge section requires documenting: known triggers/challenging contexts, key environmental accommodations, signals that support is needed, accessible/preferred regulation-strategy types (Sensory Motor / Language-Information / Reflecting-Planning), "Go To" strategies, neutral/maintenance strategies, and a three-way strategy split (every-day non-negotiable, anticipated-mismatch, and extreme-dysregulation recovery strategies) ([[raw/0825_PowerPlan_Post.extracted]]).
- The Power Down/Power UP! Levels pages first require checking physiological needs (eating, bathroom, sleep) and interoceptive needs (allergies/illness, medication, chronic pain) before applying other strategies ([[raw/0825_PowerPlan_Post.extracted]]).
- Both Power Down and Power UP! strategies are graded across three intensity levels (1, 2, 3+) and split by domain: Sensorimotor/Behavioral (Sensory, Motor) and Language/Information/Reflective/Metacognitive (Cognitive, Communication, Social) ([[raw/0825_PowerPlan_Post.extracted]]).
- Documented example Cognitive-domain strategies (identical across Power Down and Power UP! at time of source): Level 1 — ask for/give help, use Energy Meter, use Regulator 2.0; Level 2 — ask for/give break, use Energy Meter, use Regulator 2.0; Level 3+ — access to multimodal communication, visual choices of intense sensorimotor activities, give space and time for sensorimotor strategy use ([[raw/0825_PowerPlan_Post.extracted]]).
- The tool carries explicit "GOs and NOs" usage guidance framed around a Learner/Partner relationship: use for exploration and curiosity with authentic, individualized content; involve the person in creation to whatever extent is accessible; share with all trusted partners; do not use "textbook" answers, directive/compliance-forcing framing, or treat the plan as static ([[raw/0825_PowerPlan_Post.extracted]]).
- "Learner" and "Partner" are defined terms: Learners are seekers of knowledge and growth; Partners are trusted support providers who seek to understand the learner's perspective and join in the learning process ([[raw/0825_PowerPlan_Post.extracted]]).
