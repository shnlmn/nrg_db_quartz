# Action Research

## Summary

Action research is a research approach first coined by Kurt Lewin in the 1940s, referring to research that solves a pressing problem using community effort. Lewin described an iterative process of "comparative research of the conditions and effects of various forms of social action and research leading to social action." This concept represents the "northern tradition" origin of [[community-based-participatory-research|CBPR]] and [[participatory-action-research|PAR]].

## Key claims

- Kurt Lewin, a key figure in social and organizational psychology, rejected the positivist belief that researchers could "objectively" study an individual in the laboratory; he valued the study of human behavior in real-world environments from multiple perspectives (Lewin 1939) [[raw/Community-based Participatory Research (CBPR)_ Towards Equitable Involvement of Community in Psychology Research.md|Collins et al.]]
- Lewin first coined the term "action research" in the 1940s (Adelman 1993; Lewin 1946) [[raw/Community-based Participatory Research (CBPR)_ Towards Equitable Involvement of Community in Psychology Research.md|Collins et al.]]
- Lewin's work inspired many social scientists to engage in research that creates positive and lasting social change [[raw/Community-based Participatory Research (CBPR)_ Towards Equitable Involvement of Community in Psychology Research.md|Collins et al.]]
- Action research represents the "northern tradition" of CBPR, complementing the "southern tradition" from Fals Borda, Freire, and colleagues working in South America, Africa, and Asia [[raw/Community-based Participatory Research (CBPR)_ Towards Equitable Involvement of Community in Psychology Research.md|Collins et al.]]
- Action research and PAR provide significant methodological grounding for [[scandinavian-participatory-design|Scandinavian participatory design]], particularly in emphases on [[mutual-learning]] and reciprocity; there is also a broadly shared Scandinavian orientation towards action research in mainstream informatics [[raw/IJEE1353.extracted.md|Gregory 2003]]
