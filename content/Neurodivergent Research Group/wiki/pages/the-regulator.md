# The Regulator

## Summary

The Regulator is a tool in the [[energy-suite]] that catalogs regulation strategies, including "Power Packs" of strategies with a neutral effect on energy (i.e., strategies that help maintain rather than raise or lower energy levels). It is referenced by [[power-plan]] as a source for "Go To" strategies and maintenance strategies, but its own content has not yet been ingested as a primary source.

## Key claims

- [[power-plan]] references "Regulator 2.0" as a strategy option available at all three severity levels (1, 2, and 3+) of both Power Down and Power UP! cognitive strategies ([[raw/0825_PowerPlan_Post.extracted]]).
- Regulation strategies with a neutral effect on energy (maintenance strategies) are sourced from "The Regulator/Power Packs" ([[raw/0825_PowerPlan_Post.extracted]]).
- "Go To" regulation strategies that partners should be aware of are cross-referenced to an "MR/SR Worksheet" alongside the [[pic-tool]] ([[raw/0825_PowerPlan_Post.extracted]]).
