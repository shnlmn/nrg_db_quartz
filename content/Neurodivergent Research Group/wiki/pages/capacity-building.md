# Capacity Building

## Summary

Capacity building in the context of [[community-based-participatory-research|CBPR]] refers to the assessment of the strengths and needs of individuals and their communities and the provision of assistance in further developing community members', institutions', and organizations' skills, resources, and competencies. It is foundational to ensuring that research benefits and interventions are sustainable beyond any specific research project.

## Key claims

- Capacity building is how CBPR practitioners support communities' existing strengths; what it looks like varies from project to project (CTSA Community Engagement Committee Task Force 2011) [[raw/Community-based Participatory Research (CBPR)_ Towards Equitable Involvement of Community in Psychology Research.md|Collins et al.]]
- Greater community involvement leads to more productive partnerships, better research programs, and stronger implementation (Minkler et al. 2009) [[raw/Community-based Participatory Research (CBPR)_ Towards Equitable Involvement of Community in Psychology Research.md|Collins et al.]]
- CBPR entails investments in capacity building to ensure communities are better equipped to integrate and maintain interventions and support future community-based research efforts (Viswanathan et al. 2004; Souleymanov et al. 2016) [[raw/Community-based Participatory Research (CBPR)_ Towards Equitable Involvement of Community in Psychology Research.md|Collins et al.]]
- Beyond participant-level outcomes, CBPR is associated with increased capacity at the community level (Jagosh et al. 2012; Khodyakov et al. 2011) [[raw/Community-based Participatory Research (CBPR)_ Towards Equitable Involvement of Community in Psychology Research.md|Collins et al.]]
- In the LEAP case study, capacity building included hiring a meaningful activities coordinator with greatest deference to resident members' input, and residents eventually co-led and independently led programming [[raw/Community-based Participatory Research (CBPR)_ Towards Equitable Involvement of Community in Psychology Research.md|Collins et al.]]
