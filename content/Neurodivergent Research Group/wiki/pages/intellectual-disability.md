# Intellectual Disability

## Summary

Significant limitations in intellectual functioning and adaptive behaviour that manifest before the age of 18 (Schalock et al., 2010). People with intellectual disabilities are among the most marginalized in society and face considerable barriers in ensuring their perspectives contribute to public debate and research.

## Key claims

- Defined by the presence of significant limitations in intellectual functioning and adaptive behaviour manifesting before age 18; distinct from acquired brain injury, dementia, or specific learning difficulties such as dyslexia [[raw/A systematic review of photovoice research methods with people with intellectual disabilities.md|Chinn & Balota]]
- People with intellectual disabilities often need support with verbal communication, making traditional qualitative methods challenging; visual methods like [[photovoice]] offer alternative means of self-representation [[raw/A systematic review of photovoice research methods with people with intellectual disabilities.md|Chinn & Balota]]
- People with intellectual disabilities have traditionally been excluded from the research process, including those with profound and multiple intellectual disabilities [[raw/A systematic review of photovoice research methods with people with intellectual disabilities.md|Chinn & Balota]]
- Research adaptations needed include: support with memory and attention, communication access, physical dexterity accommodations, and recognition of restricted life experiences and limited opportunities to learn skills [[raw/A systematic review of photovoice research methods with people with intellectual disabilities.md|Chinn & Balota]]
- People with intellectual disabilities are commonly socialised into positions of dependence with default expectations to "ask the staff" rather than take action themselves, creating barriers to [[empowerment-in-research|empowerment]] and self-determination [[raw/A systematic review of photovoice research methods with people with intellectual disabilities.md|Chinn & Balota]]
- Inclusive research with this group means research done *with* rather than *on* people with intellectual disabilities, where academic researchers share power and responsibility [[raw/A systematic review of photovoice research methods with people with intellectual disabilities.md|Chinn & Balota]]
