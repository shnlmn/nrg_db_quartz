# Cornish Et Al 2023 Par Primer

## Summary

A comprehensive methods primer on participatory action research (PAR) published in *Nature Reviews Methods Primers* (2023). Authored by Flora Cornish, Nancy Breton, Ulises Moreno-Tabarez, Jenna Delgado, Mohi Rua, Ama de-Graft Aikins, and Darrin Hodgetts. The article defines PAR as an approach that prioritizes experiential knowledge for tackling problems caused by unequal social systems, and sets out design considerations through six building blocks, key challenges, and counter-strategies.

## Key claims

- PAR involves the participation and leadership of those people experiencing issues, who take action to produce emancipatory social change through conducting systematic research to generate new knowledge [[raw/Participatory action research - Nature Reviews Methods Primers.md|Cornish et al.]]
- Six building blocks for PAR project design: (1) building relationships; (2) establishing working practices; (3) establishing a common understanding of the issue; (4) observing, gathering and generating materials; (5) collaborative analysis; (6) planning and taking action [[raw/Participatory action research - Nature Reviews Methods Primers.md|Cornish et al.]]
- Key challenges faced by PAR projects: mismatches with institutional research infrastructure; risks of [[co-option-in-research|co-option]]; power inequalities; and the decentralizing of control [[raw/Participatory action research - Nature Reviews Methods Primers.md|Cornish et al.]]
- Counter-strategies: build PAR-friendly networks of people and infrastructures; cultivate a critical community to hold researchers to account; use critical [[reflexivity]]; redistribute powers; and learn to trust the process [[raw/Participatory action research - Nature Reviews Methods Primers.md|Cornish et al.]]
- PAR's societal contribution and methodological development can best be advanced by engaging with contemporary social movements that demand redressing inequities and recognizing situated expertise [[raw/Participatory action research - Nature Reviews Methods Primers.md|Cornish et al.]]
- [[collaborative-analysis]] involves multiple team members in the analysis and interpretation of materials generated, typically in iterative cycles of individual or pair work and group discussion [[raw/Participatory action research - Nature Reviews Methods Primers.md|Cornish et al.]]
- The primer draws on intellectual traditions including [[paulo-freire|Freire]], decolonization studies (Smith, Chilisa, Fanon), intersectionality (Crenshaw), feminist scholarship (Mohanty, Mama), and action research (Lewin) [[raw/Participatory action research - Nature Reviews Methods Primers.md|Cornish et al.]]
