# Future Workshop

## Summary

A participatory method originally developed by Robert Jungk and Norbert Mullert (1981) to involve citizens in public decision-making processes such as city planning. Adopted by [[scandinavian-participatory-design|Participatory Design]] as a structured approach for co-designers to critique current situations, envision alternatives, and develop joint action plans for change through five phases: preparation, critique, fantasy, realisation, and follow-up.

## Key claims

- The method's basic principle — enabling participants to have a say — made it of interest to PD (Kensing & Madsen 1991) [[raw/Participatory_Design_and_Design_for_Valu.extracted.md|van der Velden & Mortberg]]
- Five phases: (1) preparation — designing a welcoming atmosphere, introducing method and theme; (2) critique — generating critique of current situation using post-it notes, organising into categories, prioritising; (3) fantasy — brainstorming without restrictions, turning critique into positive ideas; (4) realisation — reviewing visions, discussing implementation possibilities, creating a joint action plan; (5) follow-up [[raw/Participatory_Design_and_Design_for_Valu.extracted.md|van der Velden & Mortberg]]
- Through involvement of various stakeholders, multiple values and views emerge out of the activities — visible during generation of critique and visions, systematisation, prioritisation, and consideration of possibilities for realisation [[raw/Participatory_Design_and_Design_for_Valu.extracted.md|van der Velden & Mortberg]]
- Future workshops for technology design produce action plans that form the first step in materialisation of requirements in the use-centred design cycle [[raw/Participatory_Design_and_Design_for_Valu.extracted.md|van der Velden & Mortberg]]
- Used in homecare practice design (Jansson 2007; Jansson & Mortberg 2011) to involve multiple occupational groups in defining demands for a digital artefact and new work practices [[raw/Participatory_Design_and_Design_for_Valu.extracted.md|van der Velden & Mortberg]]
