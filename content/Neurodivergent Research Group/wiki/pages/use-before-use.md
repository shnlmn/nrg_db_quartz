# Use Before Use

## Summary

A core concept in [[scandinavian-participatory-design|Participatory Design]] coined by Pelle Ehn (2008) describing the central challenge that the use of a designed object can only be envisioned or anticipated during the design process because there is no actual artefact to use yet. PD methods address this by engaging co-designers in telling, making, and enacting future use scenarios, enabling values and definitions of use to emerge iteratively.

## Key claims

- PD is characterised as "designing for use-before-use" (Ehn 2008): during the design process, co-designers must anticipate and explore how an object will be used before it exists [[raw/Participatory_Design_and_Design_for_Valu.extracted.md|van der Velden & Mortberg]]
- Use-before-use requires participatory methods that engage co-designers in telling (narratives, scenarios), making (prototypes, mock-ups), and enacting (role-play, walk-throughs) use (Brandt et al. 2012) [[raw/Participatory_Design_and_Design_for_Valu.extracted.md|van der Velden & Mortberg]]
- During use-before-use, values are expressed and explored and then become materialised in the form of an object; co-designers become users and the material object becomes a product or service through this iterative process [[raw/Participatory_Design_and_Design_for_Valu.extracted.md|van der Velden & Mortberg]]
- The designed object's inscriptions (choices in materials, options for use, colour, flexibility) make ideas of what is good (values), useful (functions), and beautiful (aesthetics) durable — but these inscriptions are not prescriptive; whether and how an artefact is used depends on inscription strength (Latour 1991) [[raw/Participatory_Design_and_Design_for_Valu.extracted.md|van der Velden & Mortberg]]
- In the use-oriented design approach (Bratteteig et al. 2012), the design process enables emergence of values and definitions of use, while the artefact in its different stages enables exploration of those definitions (Redstrom 2008) [[raw/Participatory_Design_and_Design_for_Valu.extracted.md|van der Velden & Mortberg]]
