# Vaughn Jacquez 2020 Choice Points

## Summary

Vaughn, L.M. & Jacquez, F. (2020). "Participatory Research Methods – Choice Points in the Research Process." *Journal of Participatory Research Methods*, 1(1). This article introduces a model of "choice points" — decision junctures at each step of the research cycle where academic-community partnerships must decide which tools and methods will produce the desired level of participation. It also provides a comprehensive taxonomy of participatory research frameworks across disciplines and articulates a [[participation-continuum]] from inform to empower.

## Key claims

- Participatory research (PR) is defined as an umbrella term for research designs, methods, and frameworks that use systematic inquiry in direct collaboration with those affected by the issue being studied for the purpose of action or change (Cargo & Mercer, 2008) [[raw/Participatory Research Methods – Choice Points in the Research Process _ Published in Journal of Participatory Research Methods.md|Vaughn & Jacquez]]
- PR prioritizes co-constructing research through partnerships between researchers and stakeholders, community members, or others with insider knowledge and lived expertise (Jagosh et al., 2012) [[raw/Participatory Research Methods – Choice Points in the Research Process _ Published in Journal of Participatory Research Methods.md|Vaughn & Jacquez]]
- The "choice points" model (Figure 1) identifies the intersection between participation level and steps in the research cycle; at each phase, decisions must be made about which tools and methods will produce the desired level of participation [[raw/Participatory Research Methods – Choice Points in the Research Process _ Published in Journal of Participatory Research Methods.md|Vaughn & Jacquez]]
- There is no prescription for the "right" way to do PR; research partners must collaborate to prioritize what is most important and choose methods that best represent stakeholder interests and maximize real-world impact [[raw/Participatory Research Methods – Choice Points in the Research Process _ Published in Journal of Participatory Research Methods.md|Vaughn & Jacquez]]
- Research tools and methods can vary in degree of participation: the same method (e.g., focus groups) can operate at different levels of the [[participation-continuum]] depending on how it is implemented [[raw/Participatory Research Methods – Choice Points in the Research Process _ Published in Journal of Participatory Research Methods.md|Vaughn & Jacquez]]
- [[community-advisory-board]]s tend to operate at the "involve" level, with community members providing feedback throughout the research process [[raw/Participatory Research Methods – Choice Points in the Research Process _ Published in Journal of Participatory Research Methods.md|Vaughn & Jacquez]]
- At the "collaborate" and "empower" levels, non-academic co-researchers share decision-making and co-leadership in the research structure itself [[raw/Participatory Research Methods – Choice Points in the Research Process _ Published in Journal of Participatory Research Methods.md|Vaughn & Jacquez]]
- Although many methods are participatory by design, conventional methods like surveys and focus groups can be adapted to be participatory if chosen and/or utilized collaboratively between academic and community partners [[raw/Participatory Research Methods – Choice Points in the Research Process _ Published in Journal of Participatory Research Methods.md|Vaughn & Jacquez]]
- The potential for immediate and sustainable impact and social change rises with increasing stakeholder participation in the research process [[raw/Participatory Research Methods – Choice Points in the Research Process _ Published in Journal of Participatory Research Methods.md|Vaughn & Jacquez]]
- The paper catalogues 25+ PR frameworks across disciplines (Table 1), including [[participatory-action-research]], [[community-based-participatory-research]], citizen science, appreciative inquiry, user-centered design research, [[decolonization-in-research|decolonizing methodologies]], emancipatory research, and others — all sharing the value of doing research *with* rather than *on* people [[raw/Participatory Research Methods – Choice Points in the Research Process _ Published in Journal of Participatory Research Methods.md|Vaughn & Jacquez]]
- Explicit description of participatory research methods, tools, and processes — along with documentation of challenges and facilitators — is often minimal to non-existent in the literature, weakening PR's broader impact [[raw/Participatory Research Methods – Choice Points in the Research Process _ Published in Journal of Participatory Research Methods.md|Vaughn & Jacquez]]
