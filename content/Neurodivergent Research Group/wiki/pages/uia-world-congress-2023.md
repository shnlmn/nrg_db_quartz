# UIA World Congress 2023

## Summary

The UIA World Congress of Architects 2023 was held in Copenhagen, Denmark, July 2–6, 2023. Organised by the International Union of Architects (UIA), it convened architects from around the world to explore how architecture responds to all 17 UN Sustainable Development Goals (SDGs).

## Science Track

For over two years preceding the congress, an international Scientific Committee analyzed architecture's responses to the SDGs, resulting in six science panels:

1. Design for Climate Adaptation
2. Design for Rethinking Resources
3. Design for Resilient Communities
4. Design for Health
5. Design for Inclusivity
6. Design for Partnerships for Change

296 papers were accepted to present from more than 750 submissions across 77 countries.
