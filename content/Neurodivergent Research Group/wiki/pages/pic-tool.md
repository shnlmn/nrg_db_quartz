# PIC Tool

## Summary

The PIC Tool is a tool in the [[energy-suite]] used to identify known triggers, challenging contexts, and environmental accommodations, and to anticipate mismatches between a person's profile and their environment or activities. Its output feeds directly into [[power-plan]], whose Power Down and Power UP! Levels sections are themselves headed "PIC - The Plan Power Down Levels" and "PIC - The Plan Power UP! Levels."

## Key claims

- [[power-plan]] is created partly from work with the PIC Tool, used to identify anticipated mismatches between a person's profile and their environment/activity ([[raw/0825_PowerPlan_Post.extracted]]).
- Known triggers/challenging contexts and key environmental accommodations in the Power Plan are both sourced by referencing the PIC Tool ([[raw/0825_PowerPlan_Post.extracted]]).
- The Power Plan's leveled Power Down/Power UP! strategy tables are themselves presented under "PIC" headings, suggesting the PIC Tool and the Power Plan's leveling structure are closely integrated or share a template ([[raw/0825_PowerPlan_Post.extracted]]).
