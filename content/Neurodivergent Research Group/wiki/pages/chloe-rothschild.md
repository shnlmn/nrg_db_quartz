# Chloe Rothschild

## Summary

Chloe Rothschild is an autistic educational consultant and self-advocate who speaks about needing multiple communication options, including communication boards, alongside her spoken language. She describes moments of dysregulation as blocking the pathway from her brain to spoken words, making backup AAC options like communication boards essential rather than optional.

## Key claims

- "I use spoken words to communicate, so people often assume I don't need alternative methods. However, I don't always have access to my spoken words. Sometimes, especially during times of dysregulation, the pathway that carries spoken words from my brain to my mouth becomes blocked. When this happens, I get stuck. In those moments, having multiple communication options such as communication boards and other forms of AAC is beneficial, supportive, and effective." [[raw/Communication_Boards.extracted.md]]
