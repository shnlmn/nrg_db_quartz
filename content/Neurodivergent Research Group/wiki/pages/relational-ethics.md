# Relational Ethics

## Summary

An approach to ethical conduct that situates ethics as ongoingly negotiated within the context of respectful relationships, beyond following the procedural rules often set out by ethics committees. In [[participatory-action-research|PAR]], relational ethics underpins the commitment to building genuine, sustained relationships with communities rather than treating ethical obligations as checklists.

## Key claims

- Relational ethics goes beyond procedural compliance with institutional ethics committees; it requires ongoing negotiation within the context of respectful relationships [[raw/Participatory action research - Nature Reviews Methods Primers.md|Cornish et al.]]
- In PAR, relational ethics means that ethical conduct is not a one-time approval but a continuous process of attending to the well-being, autonomy, and dignity of co-researchers and community members throughout the research [[raw/Participatory action research - Nature Reviews Methods Primers.md|Cornish et al.]]
- Relational ethics aligns with the broader PAR commitment to building relationships as the first of six building blocks for project design [[raw/Participatory action research - Nature Reviews Methods Primers.md|Cornish et al.]]
