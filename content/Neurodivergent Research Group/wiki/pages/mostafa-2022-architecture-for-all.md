# Mostafa 2022 Architecture for All

## Summary

Short position piece by [[magda-mostafa|Magda Mostafa]] (published 2022-02-06, IQD Magazine / autism.archi) articulating her equity principle for architectural practice and contextualising ASPECTSS® within the shifting discourse around autism identity.

## Key Claims

- ~1.5% of the world's population (~120 million individuals) exist somewhere on the autism spectrum [[raw/Autism Design and an Architecture for All_ Designing for a Differently Abled World]]
- The historical view of autism as a monolithic, pathologized condition to be cured is giving way to a strength-based, identity-first position [[raw/Autism Design and an Architecture for All_ Designing for a Differently Abled World]]
- The peak cohort of individuals first diagnosed during the early-2000s awareness surge are now reaching adulthood; self-advocacy has become an increasingly heard voice [[raw/Autism Design and an Architecture for All_ Designing for a Differently Abled World]]
- Mostafa's equity principle: "no one has the right to exist more comfortably, safely or effectively in space than anyone else" — architects are responsible for affording comfort, safety, and efficacy to the entire spectrum of the human condition [[raw/Autism Design and an Architecture for All_ Designing for a Differently Abled World]]
- "Architecture must listen" — imperative for the built environment profession to attend to self-advocacy voices [[raw/Autism Design and an Architecture for All_ Designing for a Differently Abled World]]
