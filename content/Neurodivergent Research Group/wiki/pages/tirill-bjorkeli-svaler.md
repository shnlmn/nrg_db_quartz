# Tirill Bjørkeli Svaler

## Summary

Tirill Bjørkeli Svaler is a researcher affiliated with the Faculty of Social Sciences, Oslo Metropolitan University, Norway. Known for 2023 article exploring how libraries and museums can become more accessible for autistic users.

## Work

- **Svaler (2023)**: "On making libraries and museums more accessible for autistic people" — survey (n=126), case study, and literature review on [[alm-accessibility-autism|ALM accessibility for autism]]; identifies sensory overload, social interaction, and anxiety as top barriers; proposes [[sensory-friendly-programming]]. See [[svaler-2023-alm-autism-accessibility]].
