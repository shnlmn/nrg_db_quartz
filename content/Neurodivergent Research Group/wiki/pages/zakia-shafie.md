# Zakia Shafie

## Summary

Zakia Shafie is an Egyptian architect and academic noted as one of the first female architects in Egypt and the first female chair of an architectural department in the Middle East. She served as doctoral thesis advisor to [[magda-mostafa|Magda Mostafa]] at Cairo University.

## Role in Autism Design Research

When Mostafa sought to pivot her PhD dissertation from its original topic to autism architecture — discarding 18 months of prior work — Shafie supported the decision. Mostafa describes her as "wise and wonderful" and credits Shafie's agreement as essential to the research that became the ASPECTSS® Design Index [[raw/An Interview with Magda Mostafa_ Pioneer in Autism Design.md]].
