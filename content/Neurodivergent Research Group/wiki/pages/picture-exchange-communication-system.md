# Picture Exchange Communication System

## Summary

The Picture Exchange Communication System (PECS) is a specific augmentative-and-alternative-communication protocol in which an individual exchanges pictures with a communication partner to convey a message. It is a paper-based, rapid-access form of [[augmentative-alternative-communication]] commonly associated with autistic children, though it often focuses primarily on fringe vocabulary and does not, on its own, build into a robust language system.
