# Paulo Freire

## Summary

Brazilian educator and philosopher (1921–1997) whose work on critical pedagogy and emancipatory education is foundational to participatory action research. His concept of praxis—the indissoluble unity of reflection and action—provides the epistemological basis for PAR methodology.

## Key claims

- Freire used [[participatory-action-research|PAR]] to encourage poor and deprived communities to examine and analyse the structural reasons for their oppression [[raw/Participatory action research.md|Baum et al.]]
- His epistemology rejects both the view that consciousness is a copy of external reality and the solipsist argument that the world is a creation of consciousness; human consciousness brings a [[critical-reflection]] on material reality [[raw/Participatory action research.md|Baum et al.]]
- Freire's concept of praxis holds that action and reflection are indissolubly united: "reflection and action on the world in order to transform it" [[raw/Participatory action research.md|Baum et al.]]
- "Reflection without action is sheer verbalism or armchair revolution and action without reflection is pure activism, or action for action's sake" (*Pedagogy of the Oppressed*, 1972) [[raw/Participatory action research.md|Baum et al.]]
- Through praxis, critical consciousness develops so that people cease to see their situation as "a dense, enveloping reality or a blind alley" and instead as "an historical reality susceptible of transformation" [[raw/Participatory action research.md|Baum et al.]]
- The concept of conscientização — generation of critical consciousness about social conditions beyond individual experience — is operationalised in [[photovoice]] through the [[showed-method|SHOWeD]] group discussion technique, though a [[chinn-balota-2024-photovoice-intellectual-disabilities|systematic review]] found this critical dialogue aim is difficult to achieve with people with [[intellectual-disability|intellectual disabilities]] due to communication, memory, and attention demands, and structural socialisation into dependence [[raw/A systematic review of photovoice research methods with people with intellectual disabilities.md|Chinn & Balota]]
- Freire's pedagogy for critical consciousness and liberation was a contributing influence to the [[scandinavian-participatory-design|Scandinavian participatory design]] movement, alongside post-war industrial democracy movements and trade union collaboration [[raw/IJEE1353.extracted.md|Gregory 2003]]

## Key works

- *Pedagogy of the Oppressed* (1972)
- *Education: The Practice of Freedom* (1976)
