# Epistemic Enablement

**Summary** — Epistemic enablement (Catala et al., 2021) describes the conditions and environments that actively support people in meaningfully contributing their knowledge and perspectives from their own position. Rather than viewing knowledge production as purely individual, epistemic enablement frames it as relational and contextual—shaped by the supportive structures researchers and institutions put in place.

## Key claims

- Epistemic enablement shifts focus from identifying epistemic injustice to actively creating the conditions that foster epistemic agency [[raw/dark-2025-inclusion-by-design-a-neuro-cognitive-trait-interaction-approach-to-neurodivergent-research.extracted.md|Dark 2025]]
- Knowledge production is relational and contextual, not purely individual; environments must be actively shaped to support diverse ways of knowing [[raw/dark-2025-inclusion-by-design-a-neuro-cognitive-trait-interaction-approach-to-neurodivergent-research.extracted.md|Dark 2025]]
- In neurodivergent research, epistemic enablement means designing research environments that adapt to participants' processing needs rather than expecting participants to adapt to dominant neurotypical norms [[raw/dark-2025-inclusion-by-design-a-neuro-cognitive-trait-interaction-approach-to-neurodivergent-research.extracted.md|Dark 2025]]
- Responsive research environments that accommodate communication preferences, sensory needs, cognitive processing styles, and emotional safety actively enable neurodivergent epistemic participation [[raw/dark-2025-inclusion-by-design-a-neuro-cognitive-trait-interaction-approach-to-neurodivergent-research.extracted.md|Dark 2025]]
