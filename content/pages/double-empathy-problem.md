# Double Empathy Problem

## Summary

The double empathy problem describes the bidirectional miscommunication that arises when neurodivergent individuals interact with non-neurodivergent individuals. Rather than framing communication difficulties as a deficit of the neurodivergent person alone, it recognizes that both parties may struggle to understand each other.

## Key claims

- In user research, the double empathy problem means that questions which seem clear to researchers may be overly broad or ambiguous for neurodivergent participants [[raw/3 tips for designing user research for neurodivergent testers|AbilityNet]]
- Vague prompts like "Tell me about yourself?" should be replaced with precise, specific language to prevent information overload [[raw/3 tips for designing user research for neurodivergent testers|AbilityNet]]
