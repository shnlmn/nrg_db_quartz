# ENVIDES

## Summary

Environmental Description Survey (ENVIDES) is a data collection technique using sentence stems to help participants describe the physical setting, qualities, and meanings of a place. Participants complete open-ended phrases, then quantify their experience descriptions on 1-10 scales.

## Key claims

- ENVIDES uses incomplete sentence stems (e.g., "this view...", "this place...") that participants complete with as many or as few responses as they have experiences to describe [[raw/fpsyg-15-1303397.extracted.md]]
- The technique includes seven stems in shortened form, selected to allow maximum description variety with minimal response burden [[raw/fpsyg-15-1303397.extracted.md]]
- Like RADES, participants classify responses by temporal awareness (noticed first, noticed while surveying) and importance (important vs. most important) [[raw/fpsyg-15-1303397.extracted.md]]
- Participants transform their descriptions into quantified ratings (1-10 scale) through self-generated questions (e.g., "How much do I like...?" "How much did I attentively observe...?") [[raw/fpsyg-15-1303397.extracted.md]]
- While RADES focuses on the person's bodily/emotional responses, ENVIDES focuses on the place itself and its perceived qualities [[raw/fpsyg-15-1303397.extracted.md]]

## Distinction from RADES

RADES and ENVIDES are complementary: RADES captures experiential responses (feelings, bodily sensations, actions) while ENVIDES captures environmental characteristics and appraisals (what the place is like, what it affords).
