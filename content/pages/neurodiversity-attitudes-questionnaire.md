# Neurodiversity Attitudes Questionnaire

## Summary

The Neurodiversity Attitudes Questionnaire (NDAQ) is the first instrument designed to specifically assess attitudes toward the neurodiversity perspective broadly (not limited to a single condition like autism). Developed by Schuck, Choi, Baiden, Dwyer, and Uljarević, it was validated with 351 helping professionals and fits a five-factor structure.

## Key claims

- The NDAQ uses a 6-point Likert-type scale (strongly disagree to strongly agree, with no neutral option); the neutral option was removed because participants chose it for non-systematic reasons [[raw/The Neurodiversity Attitudes Questionnaire_ Development and Initial Validation|NDAQ paper]]
- Five factors identified via exploratory structural equation modeling (ESEM): **Diversity & Inclusion**, **Fitting In**, **Cross-Neurotype Interactions**, **Medical Model**, and **Listening & Reflecting** [[raw/The Neurodiversity Attitudes Questionnaire_ Development and Initial Validation|NDAQ paper]]
- Overall reliability is very good (ω = 0.88); factor-level reliability ranges from adequate to very good (ω = 0.61–0.79) [[raw/The Neurodiversity Attitudes Questionnaire_ Development and Initial Validation|NDAQ paper]]
- NDAQ scores negatively correlate with autism/ADHD/dyslexia stigma and positively correlate with knowledge scales, supporting convergent validity [[raw/The Neurodiversity Attitudes Questionnaire_ Development and Initial Validation|NDAQ paper]]
- People who identify as neurodivergent score higher on the NDAQ than those who do not [[raw/The Neurodiversity Attitudes Questionnaire_ Development and Initial Validation|NDAQ paper]]
- Development involved a mixed neurotype team, including cognitive interviews with neurodivergent individuals and an extensive response process evaluation (RPE) [[raw/The Neurodiversity Attitudes Questionnaire_ Development and Initial Validation|NDAQ paper]]
- 28 items in the final pilot version (one item about medication was removed as many neurodiversity proponents support medication when desired) [[raw/The Neurodiversity Attitudes Questionnaire_ Development and Initial Validation|NDAQ paper]]
