# Relational Inclusion

**Summary** — Relational inclusion is the principle that inclusion is fundamentally enacted through relationships and trust, not merely through policies, structures, or accessibility checklists. It emphasizes that genuine inclusion requires ongoing relational work: building trust, negotiating power, fostering emotional safety, and maintaining mutual understanding throughout the research process.

## Key claims

- Inclusion is fundamentally relational; it is enacted through relationships, not just through inclusive structures or policy documents [[raw/dark-2025-inclusion-by-design-a-neuro-cognitive-trait-interaction-approach-to-neurodivergent-research.extracted.md|Dark 2025]]
- Meaningful inclusion requires attending to how it *feels* to be included—whether the environment fosters emotional safety, sensory comfort, and interpersonal respect [[raw/dark-2025-inclusion-by-design-a-neuro-cognitive-trait-interaction-approach-to-neurodivergent-research.extracted.md|Dark 2025]]
- Relational inclusion involves reflexive work on trust-building, power negotiation, and mutual understanding throughout the research process [[raw/dark-2025-inclusion-by-design-a-neuro-cognitive-trait-interaction-approach-to-neurodivergent-research.extracted.md|Dark 2025]]
- Research with people with lived experiences of exclusion requires prioritising relational work; institutions that prioritise individual prestige, funding, and productivity create barriers to genuine inclusion [[raw/dark-2025-inclusion-by-design-a-neuro-cognitive-trait-interaction-approach-to-neurodivergent-research.extracted.md|Dark 2025]]
- Relational inclusion brings together autism embodiment (recognizing bodily/emotional dimensions), epistemic enablement (supporting knowledge contribution), and psychological safety into practice [[raw/dark-2025-inclusion-by-design-a-neuro-cognitive-trait-interaction-approach-to-neurodivergent-research.extracted.md|Dark 2025]]
