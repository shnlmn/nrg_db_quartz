# Parker et al. 2024 Sensory Methodology

**Citation:** Parker, M., Spennemann, D. H. R., & Bond, J. (2024). The identification and documentation of on-site sensory and multisensory experience–a methodological protocol. *Field Methods*, 37(2), 128–149. https://doi.org/10.1177/1525822X241254287

## Summary

Comprehensive methodological protocol for identifying and documenting on-site sensory and multisensory experiences across five sensory modalities (aural, visual, olfactory, tactile, gustatory). Developed through heritage research but applicable across tourism, religion, marketing, and social science domains. Presents standardized methods for each sense, benefits/limitations inventory, decision tree for survey planning, and transparent data collection protocol.

## Key Claims

- **Individual sensory modalities have established methodologies at varying levels of standardization:**
  - Aural: formal ISO TS12913-2 standard for soundscape documentation [[raw/parker-et-al-2024-the-identification-and-documentation-of-on-site-sensory-and-multisensory-experience-a-methodological.extracted.md]]
  - Visual: photography and video documentation well-established [[raw/parker-et-al-2024-the-identification-and-documentation-of-on-site-sensory-and-multisensory-experience-a-methodological.extracted.md]]
  - Olfactory: smellwalk methods emerging but no formal standard [[raw/parker-et-al-2024-the-identification-and-documentation-of-on-site-sensory-and-multisensory-experience-a-methodological.extracted.md]]
  - Tactile: minimal existing discussion of documentation methods [[raw/parker-et-al-2024-the-identification-and-documentation-of-on-site-sensory-and-multisensory-experience-a-methodological.extracted.md]]
  - Gustatory: little discussion of in-situ taste documentation [[raw/parker-et-al-2024-the-identification-and-documentation-of-on-site-sensory-and-multisensory-experience-a-methodological.extracted.md]]

- **Multisensory integration differs from single-sense perception:**
  - Neural integration of multiple sense stimuli creates qualitatively different experience than isolated stimuli [[raw/parker-et-al-2024-the-identification-and-documentation-of-on-site-sensory-and-multisensory-experience-a-methodological.extracted.md]]
  - Multisensory research typically limited to few senses or purely theoretical; lacks detailed identification/documentation methodologies [[raw/parker-et-al-2024-the-identification-and-documentation-of-on-site-sensory-and-multisensory-experience-a-methodological.extracted.md]]

- **Survey design balances sensory richness with practical constraints:**
  - [[sensory-transect]] methods capture spatial transformation of sensory experience; point measurements provide comparative data [[raw/parker-et-al-2024-the-identification-and-documentation-of-on-site-sensory-and-multisensory-experience-a-methodological.extracted.md]]
  - Five separate sensewalks provide focused identification but create time lags between multisensory experiences; multisensory transects sacrifice individual sense focus for integrated experience capture [[raw/parker-et-al-2024-the-identification-and-documentation-of-on-site-sensory-and-multisensory-experience-a-methodological.extracted.md]]

- **Temporal variation critically affects sensory perception:**
  - Diurnal factors: lighting, temperature, time-dependent activities, crowd flux, meal-specific cooking, drinking practices [[raw/parker-et-al-2024-the-identification-and-documentation-of-on-site-sensory-and-multisensory-experience-a-methodological.extracted.md]]
  - Seasonal factors: temperature variation, foliage, tourist flux, seasonal foods, annual patterns [[raw/parker-et-al-2024-the-identification-and-documentation-of-on-site-sensory-and-multisensory-experience-a-methodological.extracted.md]]
  - Intra-period events (weekday/weekend): affect crowd behavior, visitor excitement [[raw/parker-et-al-2024-the-identification-and-documentation-of-on-site-sensory-and-multisensory-experience-a-methodological.extracted.md]]

- **Physiological and cultural biases in human sensory perception require mitigation:**
  - Physiological parameters (proximity to stimuli, olfactory acuity, tactile sensitivity, taste perception) affect degree of sensory contact [[raw/parker-et-al-2024-the-identification-and-documentation-of-on-site-sensory-and-multisensory-experience-a-methodological.extracted.md]]
  - Mental parameters (cultural background, localness, attention given) affect detection and identification [[raw/parker-et-al-2024-the-identification-and-documentation-of-on-site-sensory-and-multisensory-experience-a-methodological.extracted.md]]
  - Dual researchers (local + nonlocal) can overcome individual biases; physiological testing (smell/taste/touch tests) recommended pre-study [[raw/parker-et-al-2024-the-identification-and-documentation-of-on-site-sensory-and-multisensory-experience-a-methodological.extracted.md]]

- **Mixed methods approach balances human and electronic measurement:**
  - Electronic/digital tools provide robustness but lack human interpretation of context and multisensory integration [[raw/parker-et-al-2024-the-identification-and-documentation-of-on-site-sensory-and-multisensory-experience-a-methodological.extracted.md]]
  - Human engagement essential for understanding place contextualization and social meaning [[raw/parker-et-al-2024-the-identification-and-documentation-of-on-site-sensory-and-multisensory-experience-a-methodological.extracted.md]]
  - E-nose technology subordinate to human olfactory assessment; may have false positives [[raw/parker-et-al-2024-the-identification-and-documentation-of-on-site-sensory-and-multisensory-experience-a-methodological.extracted.md]]

## Open Questions

- How can tactile documentation methods be further developed and standardized? Paper identifies this as underdeveloped compared to aural/visual.
- What are best practices for gustatory documentation when iconic food products are unavailable or varies seasonally?
- How does [[smell-habituation]] affect longitudinal studies of olfactory change over months/years?
- How do different documentation approaches (subjective/objective) affect data comparability across research sites?
