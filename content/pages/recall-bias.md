# Recall Bias

## Summary

Recall bias occurs when research participants' ability to accurately remember past experiences affects the validity of research results. This bias is particularly relevant to neurodivergent populations, as conditions like ADHD can affect working memory and make retrospective survey responses unreliable.

## Key claims

- Surveys and focus groups that rely on recall can produce biased results when participants have working memory challenges associated with ADHD or other neurodivergent conditions [[raw/How to Make Design Research Neurodivergent Friendly|Salesforce]]
- Real-time feedback tools mitigate recall bias by capturing experiences as they happen rather than asking participants to remember them later [[raw/How to Make Design Research Neurodivergent Friendly|Salesforce]]
- Tim Smyth's use of Slack for design research at Salesforce demonstrated how real-time channels reduce recall bias in neurodivergent employee experience research [[raw/How to Make Design Research Neurodivergent Friendly|Salesforce]]
