# Adult Sensory History

## Summary

The Adult/Adolescent Sensory History (ASH) is a standardized formal assessment tool that documents an individual's [[sensory-preferences]] and [[sensory-processing-dysfunction]] through structured interview and history-taking. It captures developmental and contextual aspects of sensory experiences.

## Key claims

- Formal assessment tool referenced in [[sensory-assessment-neurodevelopmental]] protocols [[raw/NAIT-Guide-to-Assessment-of-Sensory-Preferences-in-Adults-2022.extracted.md]]
- One of two primary formal assessment options for adult sensory evaluation alongside [[adult-adolescent-sensory-profile]]
- Available through The Spiral Foundation (thespiralfoundation.org.uk)
- Captures history and context of sensory experiences over time [[raw/NAIT-Guide-to-Assessment-of-Sensory-Preferences-in-Adults-2022.extracted.md]]
